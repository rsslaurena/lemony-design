# Maquette du site Maison Lemony

Scope: the whole site mockup in `maquette/` (accueil, composer, collection, matières, cuir, La Maison, rendez-vous, merci, entretien). Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: wealthy cigar amateurs and professional buyers; compose a cave or pick a collection, then write to Sam. Proof: Sam, his hands, the workshop, dimensions and lead time. Constraints: no live visualiser, no price, no Hermès, four-field form, stock photos labelled as placeholders.

## Direction contract

THESIS: the site is Sam's workshop notebook, not a showroom. Choosing a cave fills a "fiche de pièce" the way Sam annotates a pattern in pencil; it refuses the category's dark-club humidor page and the e-commerce product configurator.

OWN-WORLD: charte « L'Établi ». Papier ground, encre text and primary button, kraft surfaces, filet hairlines, cognac only for cotes, piece numbers and annotations. Cormorant Garamond for voice, IBM Plex Mono for measures, labels and buttons. Dimension lines with end ticks are the recurring graphic; real daylight photos full-bleed.

STORY: a visitor sees the hands and the trade first, understands it is one craftsman, 8 to 10 weeks, no price online; composes or picks a collection; sends a request that already says what they want. Sizes read as a dimensioned elevation, colourways as indicative samples, never a shop picker.

FIRST VIEWPORT: left two-thirds papier with a large Cormorant headline, a two-line lead, "Composer ma cave" (primary) and "Prendre rendez-vous" (workshop link); right third a tall full-bleed photo of hands at work, with a cognac cote line under it stating the lead time. Header slim above.

FORM: workshop notebook (carnet d'atelier) built from the brand charte, position 1 of 1; world pinned by the charte and page structure pinned by the user-approved wireframes, which outrank the surface roll. seed:4b411ebb47061eaa

Signature interaction: the fiche de pièce on the composer page fills line by line as choices are made, each value underlined by a cognac dimension line drawing left to right. Motion grammar: fade and 12px rise, 0.9s ease-out, cotes draw on scroll, slow 1.04 zoom on large photos; reduced motion respected.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
